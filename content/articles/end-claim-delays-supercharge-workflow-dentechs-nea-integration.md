## The Dentech Advantage: Seamless Integration, Superior Workflow

With the Dentech NEA Integration, there are no third-party portals or clunky workarounds. We've built this powerful capability directly into the Dentech platform you already know and trust.

### Single-Click Access

No more exporting files, saving them to your desktop, and then uploading them to a separate program. The NEA Integration is available right inside your Dentech claim workflow after the claim is created.

### Direct from the Source

Pull attachments like radiographs and perio charts directly from within Dentech. This mitigates the risk of attaching the wrong file to the wrong patient claim, ensuring accuracy and compliance.

Every attachment sent via the integration is automatically logged, giving you a clear, consolidated audit trail for every claim, all within your Dentech system. A unique NEA tracking number is auto-generated and placed on the patient's record for effortless follow-up.
